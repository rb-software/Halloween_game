Photographic PBR textures sourced from ambientCG.

All assets are released under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://ambientcg.com/license

Material pages:
- Concrete019: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete019
- Asphalt001: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Asphalt001
- Plaster001: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plaster001

The game uses the 1K JPG Color, NormalGL, Roughness, and Displacement maps.
Displacement maps are used to build subdivided, subtly displaced wall meshes.
These assets may be copied, modified, and redistributed under CC0.

Additional photographic materials downloaded for the plaza, buildings, and roofs:
- Bricks055: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Bricks055
- RoofingTiles001: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=RoofingTiles001
- PavingStones036: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=PavingStones036

These materials are also CC0 1.0 Universal. Their 1K JPG Color, NormalGL,
Roughness, and Displacement maps are used by the game. Bricks055 and
PavingStones036 also include AmbientOcclusion maps; RoofingTiles001 does not.
